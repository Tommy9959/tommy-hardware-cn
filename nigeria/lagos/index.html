Door Hardware Wholesale to Lagos: Prices, Ports, Transit

Quick answer

Door hardware from Yiwu reaches Lagos in 25-35 days by sea, arriving at Apapa or Tin Can. Locks land at roughly $4.20-4.40 per piece against a $3.00 FOB price, and coastal humidity means SS304 hinges and plated locks, not bare iron. MOQ is 50-1000 pieces per model depending on the item.

The short version

Lagos is the entry point for most Nigerian hardware imports: containers arrive at Apapa or Tin Can, clear, and then move inland by truck. Sea transit from Ningbo or Yiwu runs 25-35 days, production 15-25 days, so plan on 45-60 days from deposit to goods in your warehouse.

What sells in Lagos is driven by two things: the construction corridor and the climate.

What actually moves in Lagos

  • Iron plate mortise locks — the standard fit for Nigerian security doors. $2.50-$5.00 per piece FOB.
  • 4x3 inch butt hinges — every door takes three. SS304 for anything near the coast.
  • 50-70mm padlocks — household gates, shop shutters, motorcycles.
  • Stainless lever handles — mid-market housing and apartment projects.
  • Drawer slides and cabinet hardware — furniture workshops supplying Lekki and Ikeja estates.

Lekki, Ajah, Ikeja and the mainland corridors all build with the same door format, which is why one container mix can serve several wholesalers.

The climate decides the material

Lagos humidity sits above 80% for most of the year and the air carries salt. That has one practical consequence: zinc-plated steel fails within 12-24 months at the hinge knuckle and lock face, while SS304 lasts years. A hinge that costs $0.20 less and rusts in a year is the most expensive line in the container.

The same applies to padlocks — plastic-cover waterproof models outsell bare iron in Lagos for a reason.

Clearing at Apapa and Tin Can

Three documents decide whether the container moves or sits:

  1. Form M — opened through your bank before shipment.
  2. SONCAP — issued by an accredited inspection body, needs product details up front.
  3. PAAR — raised at the destination port before clearing.

Terminal handling, agent fees and trucking out of Apapa typically add 3-6% of cargo value. Demurrage, when documents are late, can exceed everything else in a week.

FAQ

Container or LCL? Above roughly 15 cubic metres a 20ft container is cheaper per piece. Below that, consolidate — but expect a longer, less predictable arrival.

Can I ship to Onne instead? Yes, and for eastern Nigeria it is usually better: 30-40 days direct to Onne avoids the Lagos truck leg.

What if my buyer needs goods next month? Sea freight will not make it. Air freight is only viable for samples or high-value small items.
