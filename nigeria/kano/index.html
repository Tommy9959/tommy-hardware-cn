Door Hardware Wholesale to Kano and Northern Nigeria

Quick answer

Kano is inland and dry, which changes the specification: galvanised steel lasts years here, so SS304 is optional rather than mandatory. Goods arrive through Lagos or Onne and truck north for 2-3 days. Price sensitivity is high and the volume lines are locks, hinges and padlocks sold in cartons.

The short version

Kano is the distribution centre for northern Nigeria and it behaves differently from Lagos in one important way: the climate is kind to hardware. Dry air means galvanised steel lasts years, and the whole specification can drop a grade without the buyer paying for it later.

Logistics are the trade-off. There is no seaport in the north: containers arrive at Lagos or Onne and truck north for 2-3 days.

What sells in Kano

  • Iron plate mortise locks — the standard security door lock across the north.
  • Galvanised butt hinges — 4x3 inch dominates; SS304 only for bathrooms and kitchens.
  • Padlocks — 50-63mm, the volume line in every market.
  • Basic lever and rose handles — plain finishes outsell premium ones.
  • Iron casing pipes — construction demand runs alongside the hardware trade.

Price per carton is the deciding factor in most conversations. Where a Lagos buyer asks about SS304 first, a Kano buyer asks what the galvanised option costs.

The truck leg, and what it costs you

Kano adds two costs Lagos does not have: road freight north and handling risk. Practical consequences:

  1. Cartons must be strong. Multiple transfers mean more chance of crushing; specify export-grade cartons, not retail boxes.
  2. Count at the port. Discrepancies are far easier to resolve before the truck leaves than in Kano.
  3. Plan the season. Roads north are slower in the rainy season; a container that lands in July may reach Kano later than planned.

Specification advice for the north

Where the door is Recommended Why
Exterior, residential Galvanised butt hinge Dry air, acceptable life at lower cost
Bathroom / kitchen SS304 Steam and washing, not rain
Shop shutters Heavy padlock, plated Daily use, dust
Public buildings SS304 + closer High traffic, longer replacement cycle

FAQ

Can I ship a full container to Kano? Only to a seaport first. The container is cleared and devanned at the port, then goods move north by truck.

Is the north a smaller market? It is a different market: fewer premium buyers, more volume per carton, and a specification that suits cheaper materials.
