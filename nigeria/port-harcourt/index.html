Door Hardware Wholesale to Port Harcourt via Onne Port

Quick answer

Port Harcourt is served directly through Onne port in 30-40 days, skipping the Lagos truck leg. The Niger Delta climate is the harshest in Nigeria for hardware — salt air plus heavy rain — so SS304 hinges and plated locks are the default, not an upgrade. Project buyers dominate: oil and gas facilities, hotels and residential estates.

The short version

Port Harcourt has its own port. Onne takes containers in 30-40 days from Ningbo or Yiwu, and clearing there avoids the 8-10 hour Lagos truck leg entirely. For a buyer supplying Rivers, Bayelsa, Akwa Ibom and Cross River, that is usually the better route.

The climate is the other half of the story. The Niger Delta has some of the highest humidity and rainfall in Nigeria, plus salt air. Hardware that survives Kano will fail here.

What Port Harcourt buys

  • SS304 hinges — the default, not the upgrade. 4x3 and 5x4 inch.
  • Plated mortise locks — iron plate locks with adequate plating, or stainless face plates.
  • Waterproof padlocks — plastic-cover models for outdoor gates and site storage.
  • Door closers and commercial hardware — facilities, hotels and office fit-outs.
  • Heavy-duty handles — high-traffic entrances in commercial buildings.

Oil and gas facilities and hotel projects specify higher than the residential market, which pulls the average order value up.

The corrosion question, settled

In Port Harcourt the calculation is simple:

Option Cost per hinge Realistic life (exposed)
Zinc-plated steel $0.35-$0.80 12-18 months
SS304 $0.50-$1.80 8-10 years
Hot-dip galvanised $1.00-$2.50 5-8 years

Three hinges per door, plus labour to change them, means the cheap hinge costs more inside two years. That is why Port Harcourt orders skew stainless even at a higher FOB price.

Project orders

Projects buy as a package, not a product: hinges, locks, handles, closers and stoppers in one shipment, with quantities matched to a door schedule. Send the door schedule and we quote CIF Onne with carton counts, so the site team can plan storage.

Documentation is the same as Lagos — Form M before shipment, SONCAP certificate, PAAR at the port — but at Onne the clearing agent matters more, because fewer forwarders handle it well.

FAQ

Is Onne cheaper than Lagos for the southeast? Usually yes once you count the truck leg, but confirm current port charges with your agent — they move.

Can I mix SS304 and galvanised in one order? You can, but keep one grade per door. Mixed grades corrode at different rates and the joints loosen unevenly.
