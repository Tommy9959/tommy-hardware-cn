Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What hardware sells best in Nigeria?

A: Based on 10+ years of sales data, mortise locks ($3.00-$6.00) and 50mm standard padlocks ($1.00-$3.00) are the most popular. Lever-style door handles in satin nickel also sell well, making up 75% of handle sales!

Q: What's the difference between SS201 and SS304, and which one should I choose for Nigeria?

A: SS201 is good for inland cities (Abuja, Kano), dry climate. SS304 is a must for coastal areas (Lagos, Port Harcourt), otherwise it will rust!

Q: Do you provide SONCAP and Form M support?

A: Yes! We handle 100% of SONCAP for our Nigerian clients for FREE, and we can also guide you through the Form M process!

Q: Do you accept mixed container orders and free samples?

A: Yes! We accept mixed container orders (mix different products together), and we offer free samples (you only pay for shipping)!

Q: What payment terms do you accept?

A: Standard is T/T (30% deposit, 70% against B/L copy). We can do L/C for orders over $50k.
