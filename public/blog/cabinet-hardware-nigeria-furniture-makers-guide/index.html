Cabinet Hardware for Africa: What Nigerian Furniture Makers Actually Need

In 2019, a furniture maker in Nnewi called me with a problem. He’d imported cabinet hinges from a new supplier — paid $0.18 each, thought he’d hit the jackpot. Six months later, half the hinges had seized up from rust. Customers were calling, complaining. One customer’s kitchen cabinet door fell off entirely.

“How do I fix this, Tommy?” he asked.

“You start by throwing those hinges away,” I said. “Then buy the right ones.”

He took my advice. Ordered 12,000 pieces of our mid-range hydraulic hinges at $0.48 each. Cost more upfront, but he hasn’t had a single complaint since.

This guide is everything I’ve learned about cabinet hardware for the Nigerian market — what hinges and slides actually sell, what finishes last, and how to avoid the “cheap-but-expensive” mistake.

Hinges: The Most Ordered Cabinet Hardware (45% of Sales)

1. Hydraulic / Soft-Close Hinges — The #1 Seller

This is the biggest trend I’ve seen in 10 years. Five years ago, almost nobody in Nigeria asked for soft-close hinges. Now it’s the standard.

Why soft-close took over:

  • Premium furniture makers in Lagos use it to justify higher prices (a kitchen with soft-close can sell for 30-50% more)
  • Homeowners who’ve seen it at a friend’s house want it
  • It makes cheap MDF cabinets feel more solid

What sells best:

  • 35mm cup diameter (standard)
  • Clip-on mechanism (easier installation)
  • Full overlay (most common for Nigerian kitchen cabinets)
  • Nickel plated finish (resists humidity better than painted)
  • Opening angle: 90°-110° (95° is most popular)

Price range: $0.48-$0.90 per piece FOB (cold-rolled steel hinges) MOQ: 500-1,000 pieces per type

2. Standard Clip-On Hinges — Still Popular for Budget Projects

Not everyone can afford soft-close. Budget housing projects and smaller furniture makers still prefer standard hinges.

Price range: $0.20-$0.35 per piece FOB The catch: You get what you pay for. At $0.20, the steel is thinner and the plating is basic. It’ll work for 2-3 years in a dry climate, less in coastal areas.

3. Heavy-Duty Hinges — For Commercial Use

For office cabinets, hotel rooms, and institutional projects:

  • 1.5mm-2.0mm steel thickness (vs 1.0mm-1.2mm for domestic)
  • Load rating: 30-50kg per pair
  • Price: $0.80-$1.50 per piece

Drawer Slides: The Second Most Important Item (30% of Sales)

1. Three-Fold Ball Bearing Slides — The Workhorse

Why this is the best option for Nigeria:

  • Cold-rolled steel with zinc plated finish
  • 35-45kg load capacity
  • Smooth operation even after years of use
  • Resists humidity much better than ordinary roller slides

What sells:

  • Lengths: 300mm (12"), 350mm (14"), 400mm (16"), 450mm (18")
  • 400mm and 450mm are most popular for kitchen cabinets
  • 300mm and 350mm for smaller cabinets and desks

Price range: $0.30-$0.65 per pair FOB MOQ: 500-1,000 pairs per length

2. Full Extension Slides — Growing Fast

More furniture makers want drawers that open completely. Full extension slides now account for 20% of our slide orders.

Price range: $0.70-$1.20 per pair FOB

3. Soft-Close Slides — The Premium Option

Soft-close drawer slides are still new in Nigeria, but growing fast. In 2025, 12% of our drawer slide orders were soft-close, up from 5% in 2023.

Price range: $1.50-$3.00 per pair FOB

What NOT to Buy for Nigeria

1. Ordinary roller slides (plastic wheels) I’ve seen too many furniture makers buy these for $0.15/pair. They jam after 6 months in Lagos humidity. Don’t do it.

2. Thin hinges (0.8mm steel or less) The steel warps. The door sags. The customer calls you.

3. Painted hinges (instead of nickel/zinc plated) Paint chips off in humid conditions. Plating lasts much longer.

What Finishes Work in Nigeria’s Climate

Finish Coastal (Lagos, PH) Inland (Abuja, Kano) Price Factor
Nickel plated ✅ Excellent ✅ Excellent 1x
Zinc plated + passivation ✅ Good ✅ Excellent 0.9x

My advice for most buyers: Go with nickel-plated hinges and zinc-plated drawer slides. They’re the best value-for-money in Nigeria’s climate.
