Building Materials from China to Nigeria 2026 — Tile Adhesive, Waterproofing, Wallpaper & More Wholesale Guide

If you’re in the Nigerian construction industry, you know the struggle: quality building materials are expensive locally, and finding reliable suppliers from China is a minefield of fake products and broken promises.

After 10 years of supplying building materials to Nigeria, here’s what I’ve learned about what works — and what doesn’t.

The Building Materials Gap in Nigeria

Nigeria’s construction sector is booming — housing deficit, road projects, commercial buildings everywhere. But the local manufacturing capacity can’t keep up.

Most building materials in Nigeria are imported. The problem?

Building Materials We Supply

BM-001 Tile Adhesive Standard

  • Type: Cement-based with polymer additives
  • Colors: White or Gray
  • Packing: 20kg / 25kg bags
  • Pallet: 48 bags per pallet
  • Price: $4.50 - $7.00/bag (FOB)
  • Shelf life: 12 months
  • Best for: Wall and floor tiling in residential and commercial buildings

Tip: Gray tile adhesive sells 70% more in Nigeria. White is used mainly for marble and light-colored tiles.

BM-003 Waterproof Coating

  • Type: Acrylic polymer
  • Colors: White or Clear
  • Packing: 5kg / 10kg / 20kg pails
  • Coverage: ~1kg/m² per coat
  • Price: $1.80 - $3.50/kg (FOB)
  • Shelf life: 18 months
  • Best for: Roofs, external walls, bathrooms, water tanks

Tip: Waterproofing demand in Nigeria grows 15-20% yearly. Most popular for Lagos coastal buildings.

BM-002 Ceramic Wallpaper

  • Type: Non-woven fabric, washable
  • Roll size: 0.53m × 10m (5.3m²)
  • Patterns: Solid colors, textured, floral, geometric
  • Price: $2.00 - $8.00/roll (FOB)
  • Shelf life: Indefinite (store dry)
  • Best for: Living rooms, bedrooms, hotel lobbies, offices

Tip: Solid neutral colors (beige, gray, cream) are 60% of Nigerian orders. Bold patterns sell in Lagos upscale markets.

BM-004 Wall Putty Powder

  • Type: Cement-based with polymer
  • Color: White
  • Packing: 20kg bags
  • Coverage: ~1.5kg/m²
  • Price: $3.00 - $5.00/bag (FOB)
  • Shelf life: 12 months
  • Best for: Interior wall leveling before painting or wallpapering

BM-005 Construction Adhesive

  • Type: MS Polymer hybrid
  • Color: White or Gray
  • Packing: 300ml / 600ml cartridges, 20kg pails
  • Price: $1.50 - $4.00/kg (FOB)
  • Shelf life: 12 months
  • Best for: Panel bonding, skirting, trim, general construction bonding

BM-006 PVC Ceiling Panel

  • Type: PVC + stabilizers, UV resistant
  • Size: 20cm × 6m standard (custom widths available)
  • Colors / Finishes: White, wood grain, marble pattern
  • Price: $1.50 - $3.50/sheet (FOB)
  • Shelf life: Indefinite
  • Best for: Ceilings in homes, offices, shops, hospitals

Tip: White PVC ceiling is 75% of Nigerian demand. Wood grain finish is growing — popular in Abuja and Lagos upscale homes.

Common Mistakes

❌ Not factoring in shipping costs

FOB + shipping + clearing + local transport = your total landed cost. Calculate before pricing.

❌ Wrong product for the season

Rainy season (April-October): waterproofing products are in high demand. Dry season: more construction, tile adhesive sells well.

❌ Poor storage planning

Tile adhesive and wall putty expire. Don’t order more than you can sell within 6-8 months.

❌ Ignoring SONCAP requirements

All building materials need SONCAP for Nigeria clearance. SOLA provides it free with every order.
