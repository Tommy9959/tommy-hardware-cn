Why Quality Sliding Tracks Matter in Nigeria

Sliding tracks and drawer slides are everywhere in Nigerian construction:

  • Wardrobes in new apartments
  • Kitchen drawers in modern homes
  • Sliding doors in offices and commercial buildings
  • Barn doors in upscale interiors

The problem? Cheap tracks bind, rust, or fail after just a few months in Nigeria’s climate. This means:

  • For end users: Constant repairs, broken drawers, stuck doors
  • For hardware dealers: Customer complaints, warranty headaches, lost business
  • For construction companies: Callbacks after project handover

The fix is simple: source galvanized or stainless steel sliding tracks from a factory that understands Africa’s conditions.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Buying untested products

Always request samples before placing a bulk order. SOLA offers free samples (you pay shipping only).

❌ Choosing price over quality

A $0.50 cheaper drawer slide that fails in 3 months will cost you more in customer loyalty than you save.

❌ Neglecting documentation

Without SONCAP and Form M, your container will sit at Apapa port accruing demurrage charges ($100-200/day).

❌ Ordering wrong materials for the climate

As mentioned above — stainless steel for Lagos, zinc plated for Abuja. Know your market.
