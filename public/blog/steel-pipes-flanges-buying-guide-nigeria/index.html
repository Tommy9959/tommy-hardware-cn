Zinc-coated pipes for water supply and outdoor applications:

  • Price: $700 – $1,100 per ton FOB
  • Sizes: 15mm — 100mm (1/2″ — 4″)
  • Best for: Water pipelines, fencing, scaffolding

SONCAP Certification for Steel Products

All steel pipes and flanges imported to Nigeria require SONCAP certification. Here’s the process:

  1. Product testing at an accredited lab (we handle this at our factory)
  2. Product Certificate (PC) issued by SONCAP (valid 1–3 years)
  3. SONCAP Certificate of Conformity (SCoC) for each shipment

Cost estimate: $500 – $1,500 per product category Form M + PAAR: Required for customs clearance

✅ SOLA Hardware includes SONCAP/Form M/PAAR documentation for all orders. No hidden fees.

Quality Checklist for Importers

Before placing an order, verify:

  • Material certificate (Mill Test Certificate)
  • Wall thickness tolerance (±10% max)
  • Thread quality (for threaded pipes)
  • Flange face finish (no scratches or damage)
  • Zinc coating weight (for GI pipes — minimum 200 g/m²)
  • Hydrostatic test report (for pressure-rated pipes)
  • Packing standard (bundle strapping, waterproof wrapping)
