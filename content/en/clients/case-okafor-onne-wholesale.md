## Client Background

Okafor runs a hardware wholesale business near Onne Port in Rivers State, Nigeria. With over $200K in annual turnover, he supplies building materials and hardware products to retailers and contractors across the South-South region — Port Harcourt, Owerri, Calabar, and Uyo.

For years, he sourced products through middlemen in Lagos. Prices were high, quality was inconsistent, and delivery timelines were a gamble.

## The Challenge

Okafor needed a reliable direct supplier that could cover his **full product range** — not just one or two categories. His customers expected:

- **Door locks and handles** that last in Nigeria's humid climate
- **Door hinges** strong enough for commercial use
- **Iron casing pipes** (16mm & 25mm) for construction projects
- **Sofa legs and cabinet hardware** for furniture makers
- **Flanges and building hardware** for contractors
- Competitive pricing to protect his margins
- Consistent quality across every shipment

Finding a Chinese factory that does all of this, at wholesale pricing, with reliable shipping — that was the real challenge.

> "I was tired of the middlemen. Every time prices went up, I had to explain to my customers. I knew there had to be a better way."
