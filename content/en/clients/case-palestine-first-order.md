## Client Background

A building hardware importer based in Palestine, supplying door hardware products to construction companies and hardware retailers across the Palestinian territories. They were looking for a reliable China-based supplier for door lock products suitable for the Middle East construction market.

## Challenge

The client needed high-quality door locks that meet Middle Eastern architectural standards and could withstand the local climate conditions. They needed a supplier with competitive pricing, reliable quality control, and experience shipping to the Middle East region. Finding a factory that understands both quality requirements and export logistics was their primary concern.

## Solution

SOLA Hardware provided:
- Carefully selected mortise lock models suitable for Middle Eastern door standards
- Factory direct pricing with full quality inspection
- Professional export documentation and shipping arrangement
- Dedicated communication throughout the entire process

## Results

- **First order value: ¥19,600 (RMB)**
- All products met client's quality standards
- Professional packaging ensured damage-free delivery
- Client expressed satisfaction and indicated intent for repeat orders
- Successfully opened SOLA Hardware's Palestine market
