## New Infrastructure Driving Hardware Demand

Three major projects are reshaping Nigeria's construction landscape in 2026:

**1. Lagos-Calabar Coastal Highway (700km)**
Africa's largest ongoing infrastructure project. Along the corridor, new towns and housing estates are springing up, each requiring thousands of hardware items.

**2. Abuja Master Plan Redevelopment**
The federal government's renewed focus on the capital territory is creating demand for premium residential and commercial hardware.

**3. Renewed Hope Cities & Housing Programme**
The Housing Ministry's initiative to deliver housing across all 36 states, aiming for 10,000+ units per state per year.
