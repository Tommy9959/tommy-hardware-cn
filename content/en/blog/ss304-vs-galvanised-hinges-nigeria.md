## The short version

A door needs three hinges. At mid-range prices that is **$1.50 for galvanised** (HH-003, $0.35-0.80) against **$2.10 for SS304** (HH-001, $0.50-1.20). Sixty cents apart — which is why most Nigerian buyers pick galvanised.

Then the door comes back eighteen months later.

| Over 5 years, one coastal door | Galvanised | SS304 |
|---|---|---|
| Initial hinges (3 pcs) | $1.50 | $2.10 |
| Replacements | 2-3 sets ("$4.50") | none |
| Fitting labour (₦ + downtime) | ~$6.00 | ~$2.00 |
| **Total** | **≈$10.50** | **≈$4.10** |

The numbers assume a 12-24 month galvanised life and $2 per door fitting. Change the labour rate and the gap moves; change the climate and it can disappear entirely.

## Why galvanised fails in Lagos and not in Kano

Galvanising is a zinc layer over steel. Zinc protects steel sacrificially — it corrodes first — but only while it is intact.

- **At the knuckle**, the pin rubs through the coating within months and rust starts *inside* the joint, where you cannot see it.
- **Salt air accelerates it.** Lagos, Port Harcourt and Calabar sit in coastal humidity above 80% for most of the year.
- **Inland it lasts far longer.** In Kano, Kaduna or Abuja the same hinge can run five years or more, because the air is dry and the salt is absent.

That is the whole answer to "which hinge for Nigeria": it is not one answer, it is a line drawn at the coast.

## Where the money hides

The purchase price is the visible cost. The invisible ones:

1. **Labour.** Replacing three hinges is a carpenter visit. In Lagos that is a day's work plus transport, and it happens on a door that is already in use.
2. **Complaint handling.** A developer who fits 200 doors with hinges that rust in a year does not blame the hinge. They blame the supplier.
3. **Rust stains.** Rust streaks down painted doors and onto floors; repainting costs more than the hinges ever did.
4. **Security.** A hinge that seizes or pulls out of the frame is a door that no longer closes properly.

## How to specify it properly

- **Coastal projects:** SS304 (HH-001 4"x3" or HH-004 4"x4"), minimum 2.5mm leaf thickness for external doors.
- **Inland projects:** galvanised HH-003 is fine and saves 300-400 naira per door.
- **Indoors, dry:** zinc alloy (HH-005) or aluminium (HH-006) work and are lighter.
- **Check the grade, not the shine.** SS201 is sold as "stainless" and pits in salt air within a year. Ask for SS304 and check the mill certificate if the order is large.
- **Match the screws.** Stainless hinges with plated screws give you a rust line at the screw heads; specify matching screws.

## Questions buyers ask

**Will SS304 really last eight years in Lagos?** In normal residential use, yes — SS304 resists salt air rather than merely coating the steel. On a beachfront or an industrial site, wash the hinges once a year and expect the finish to dull before the metal fails.

**Can I mix SS304 and galvanised?** You can, but the two corrode at different rates and the joint loosens unevenly. Keep one grade per door.

**Is SS201 a cheaper SS304?** It is a different alloy, not a cheaper version. It stains in coastal air and is not a substitute where salt is present.
