## Why Market Prices in Nigeria Are So Much Higher

If you walk into a hardware market in Lagos and see the same lock at ₦8,000–₦15,000 (about $5–$10), don't be surprised. Here's what's inside that markup:

- **Shipping & freight** from China to Lagos/Onitsha
- **Import duties & SONCAP certification** — up to 20–35% depending on the item
- **Trader margins** at each resale level (importer → wholesaler → retailer)
- **Demurrage & clearing fees** at the port

By the time hardware reaches a shelf in Nigeria, the price is typically 3–5× the FOB price. That's normal — but it means **the factory price is where the real savings are**, especially if you're a builder, retailer or furniture maker buying in volume.

## How to Buy Door Hardware in Nigeria Without Overpaying

### 1. Buy in Bulk (Wholesale)
Prices drop sharply with volume. A 100-piece order of door locks might be $2.80/pc; a 1,000-piece order can drop below $2.00/pc. If you're a retailer or contractor, always negotiate by volume.

### 2. Buy Direct from a China Factory
Cutting out middlemen is the single biggest saving. A factory in Yiwu or Wenzhou ships to Lagos regularly — you pay the FOB price + shipping, not the 3–5× market markup.

### 3. Choose Durability for Nigeria's Climate
This is where buyers lose money quietly. Cheap zinc or uncoated iron hardware rusts fast in Nigeria's humidity and Harmattan dust. Paying a little more for **stainless steel or galvanized** hardware now saves you from replacing it in a year.

### 4. Get SONCAP / Form M in Order
Hardware imports to Nigeria need proper certification. Work with a supplier who handles SONCAP, Form M and PAAR for you — it saves thousands of naira and avoids customs delays.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the price of a door lock in Nigeria in 2026?**
A: Market prices typically range from ₦3,500 to ₦15,000 depending on quality and location. Factory-direct (FOB China) wholesale price is $1.50–$6.00 per piece for standard iron and brass door locks.

**Q: How much do door hinges cost in Nigeria?**
A: Standard galvanized butt hinges sell for about ₦500–₦1,500 each at the market. Stainless steel SS304 hinges are more expensive at ₦1,500–₦3,500. Factory price starts from $0.20 per piece.

**Q: Where can I buy door hardware wholesale in Nigeria?**
A: Major hardware markets in Lagos (TradeFair, Oyingbo), Onitsha (Bridgehead) and Aba stock hardware. For wholesale/bulk pricing, buying direct from a China factory is cheaper.
