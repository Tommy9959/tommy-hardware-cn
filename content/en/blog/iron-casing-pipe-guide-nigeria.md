## What is Iron Casing Pipe?

Iron casing pipe (also called **conduit pipe**, **conduit tube**, or **threaded steel pipe in some contexts**) is a hollow steel tube used in construction for:

- **Embedding electrical wiring** in concrete walls and floors
- **Structural reinforcement** in concrete columns and beams
- **Protective sheathing** for plumbing and gas lines
- **Underground cable routing** for outdoor installations

In Nigeria, the two most popular sizes are:

| Size | Typical Use |
|------|-------------|
| **16mm** (½ inch) | Standard electrical wiring in walls, lighting circuits |
| **25mm** (1 inch) | Main power lines, underground conduits, heavy wiring runs |

Think of it as the bones that carry your building's nervous system — it needs to be strong, durable, and correct from day one, because once it's cast in concrete, you can't change it.

## Quality Checklist

The Nigerian market has everything from high-quality import pipe to thin-walled scrap metal. Here's how to tell the difference:

### ✅ Good Quality Pipe
- **Uniform wall thickness** — no thin spots or uneven seams
- **Clean weld seam** — smooth, no slag or holes
- **Proper galvanization** — even coating, no rust spots
- **Bends without cracking** — test bend a sample to 90°
- **Clear markings** — size, grade, and manufacturer stamped

### ❌ Poor Quality Pipe
- **Uneven wall thickness** — often thinner than advertised
- **Rough weld seam** — can damage wire insulation when pulled through
- **Rust already forming** — means poor or no galvanization
- **Cracks when bent** — brittle, substandard steel
- **Underweight** — pack is noticeably lighter than it should be

### Simple Test
Take a 1-meter sample and weigh it:
- 16mm x 1.5mm should weigh approximately **0.55-0.62 kg/m**
- 25mm x 2.0mm should weigh approximately **1.25-1.35 kg/m**

Significantly lighter? The wall thickness is thinner than specified.
