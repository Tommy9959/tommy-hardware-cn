## Steel Flanges: Standards and Specifications

Flanges are critical for connecting pipes, valves, and fittings. Here's what Nigerian importers need to know:

### Common Flange Types for Nigeria

| Flange Type | Best Application |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Weld Neck Flange | High-pressure systems |
| Slip-On Flange | General purpose, low-pressure |
| Blind Flange | Closing pipe ends |
| Socket Weld Flange | Small diameter, high pressure |
| Threaded Flange | No welding required |

### Flange Standards

**ANSI / ASME B16.5** — The most widely used standard in Nigeria
- Classes: 150, 300, 400, 600, 900, 1500, 2500
- Sizes: 1/2″ to 24″
- Facing: Raised Face (RF), Flat Face (FF), Ring Joint (RTJ)

**BS 4504 / DIN** — Common in European-sourced projects
- PN ratings: PN6, PN10, PN16, PN25, PN40
- Sizes: DN15 to DN600

**2026 Flange Price Range:** $1.50 – $8.00 per piece (FOB, depending on size, class, and material)

## SONCAP Certification for Steel Products

All steel pipes and flanges imported to Nigeria require SONCAP certification. Here's the process:

1. **Product testing** at an accredited lab (we handle this at our factory)
2. **Product Certificate (PC)** issued by SONCAP (valid 1–3 years)
3. **SONCAP Certificate of Conformity (SCoC)** for each shipment

**Cost estimate:** $500 – $1,500 per product category
**Form M + PAAR:** Required for customs clearance
