## Why Door Accessories Matter

Door accessories are small items that make a big difference:

- **Door closers** prevent doors from slamming (saves maintenance costs)
- **Door viewers** let you see who's outside (security)
- **Door stoppers** protect walls from door handles
- **Security chains** add an extra layer of protection
- **Door bolts** provide additional locking
- **Door finger guards** prevent injuries (essential for schools and offices)

In Nigeria's market, there's a huge gap between cheap (fails fast) and good quality (lasts years). **Most importers buy the wrong quality level** for their target customers.

## Quality Levels: What Nigerian Importers Should Know

### Economy Grade (Cheapest)
- $0.20 - $1.00 per item
- Thin materials, poor plating
- Lasts 3-6 months in Nigerian climate
- **Best for**: Budget housing projects, price-sensitive retailers

### Standard Grade (Best Seller)
- $1.00 - $3.00 per item
- Quality materials, good finish
- Lasts 1-3 years
- **Best for**: Most Nigerian hardware stores and construction projects

### Premium Grade
- $3.00 - $8.00 per item
- Solid brass or stainless steel
- Lasts 5-10 years
- **Best for**: Luxury homes, hotels, commercial buildings

> **SOLA's recommendation**: Most Nigerian importers do best with Standard Grade — it offers the best price-to-quality ratio and matches what the majority of customers want.

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Buying mixed quality
Don't mix cheap and good quality in one container — your customers will notice the difference and lose trust.

### ❌ Forgetting the small items
Door stoppers and angle brackets are cheap but have good margins. Don't skip them.

### ❌ Wrong material for climate
In Lagos and Port Harcourt, use stainless steel. In Abuja and Kano, zinc plated is fine.
