## Shipping & Returns Policy

*Last updated: June 18, 2026*

---

### Shipping Information

**Shipping Ports:**
- Loading port: Shanghai / Ningbo, China
- Destination: Major African ports including Lagos (Nigeria), Mombasa (Kenya), Dar es Salaam (Tanzania), Tema (Ghana)

**Shipping Methods:**
- Sea freight (LCL & FCL) — recommended for bulk orders
- Air freight — available for sample orders and urgent shipments
- Express (DHL, FedEx, UPS) — for small samples

**Delivery Timeframes:**
| Method | Estimated Time |
|--------|---------------|
| Sea freight (LCL) | 20-35 days |
| Sea freight (FCL) | 15-25 days |
| Air freight | 5-10 days |
| Express (samples) | 3-7 days |

**Shipping Documents:**
We provide full export documentation:
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
- Certificate of Origin
- SONCAP / PAAR support (for Nigeria)

### Order Processing

- Sample orders: 3-5 business days
- Bulk orders: 15-20 business days
- Custom/OEM orders: 20-30 business days (depending on complexity)

---

### Returns & Refunds

**Defective Products:**
If you receive defective or incorrect products, please contact us within 7 days of receipt. Include photos of the issue. We will:
- Replace the defective products
- Or issue a refund (at our discretion)

**Non-Defective Returns:**
Returns for non-defective products are accepted within 14 days of receipt. The buyer is responsible for return shipping costs. A 15% restocking fee applies.

**Refund Processing:**
Refunds are processed within 10 business days after we receive and inspect the returned products.

---

### Claims Process

1. Notify us within 7 days of receipt
2. Provide order number, product photos, and description of issue
3. We will respond within 24-48 hours
4. Resolution via replacement or refund
