## The short version

Onitsha is where southeast Nigeria buys hardware. The **Building Materials International Market at Ogidi**, a few minutes from the city, is the reference point: shop after shop selling locks, hinges, handles, tiles, pipes and furniture fittings, mostly in cartons, mostly to traders who then supply Anambra, Enugu, Abia, Ebonyi and beyond.

Two shipping options serve it:

- **Lagos (Apapa / Tin Can) → truck east.** 25-35 days at sea, then the inland leg.
- **Onne → truck north.** 30-40 days at sea, shorter road leg, often the better choice for a full container.

## What Onitsha restocks

The market runs on volume lines, not long-tail:

- **Iron plate mortise locks** — the default lock on Nigerian security doors, sold by the carton (20 sets).
- **4x3 inch butt hinges** — three per door, sold by the box.
- **50-70mm padlocks** — the highest-turnover item in the whole catalogue.
- **Drawer slides and cabinet hardware** — furniture workshops around Onitsha and Aba feed this.
- **Iron casing pipes and building materials** — for the construction trade that shares the same market.

## The reorder rhythm

Most Onitsha buyers run a **2-4 month cycle**: one container in, sell through, order again. Two things follow from that:

1. **Timing matters.** A container ordered in March lands in May; ordered in June, it lands after the rainy season has started and hinges sell slower.
2. **The mix matters more than the depth.** Ten fast-moving SKUs across locks, hinges and padlocks outsell one design bought deep, because the market itself is a mix of small shops.

## Price expectations

Onitsha buyers compare per carton, not per piece. At FOB level:

| Item | FOB Yiwu | Per carton |
|---|---|---|
| Door locks | $2.50-$5.00/pc | 20 sets/carton |
| Hinges | $0.35-$2.50/pc | by weight |
| Padlocks | $0.42-$2.71/pc | 100-200 pcs |
| Drawer slides | $5.00-$12.00/pc | 50-100 pcs |

Landed cost in Onitsha sits 35-45% above FOB once freight, duty, VAT and clearing are added. Full catalogue with MOQ: [price index](/price-index/).

## Working with a factory rather than a Lagos trader

Buying direct removes one margin layer but adds the clearing work. What makes it work in practice:

- **Documents before deposit.** SONCAP needs product details up front.
- **Mixed container.** Locks plus hinges plus padlocks in one 20ft keeps freight per piece low.
- **One responsible contact.** When a hinge carton arrives short, you want the factory, not three intermediaries.

## FAQ

**Can I visit before ordering?** Yes — samples can be couriered, and video calls from the factory floor are routine.

**Do you sell to shops or only importers?** Wholesale only: 50-1000 pieces per model depending on the item.

**Which is cheaper, Lagos or Onne routing?** It depends on the truck leg and the time of year. Ask for both before you fix the price to your buyers.
