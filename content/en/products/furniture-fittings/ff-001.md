### Material and finish

Aluminium alloy is light, does not rust, and finishes cleanly in anodised silver or powder-coated black. It is the right choice where weight matters — wardrobe rails, pull handles and furniture legs — but bare polished aluminium dulls in humid air, so anodising is not optional.
