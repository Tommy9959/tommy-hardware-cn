## Who buys the FF-001

FF-001 is a straight aluminium bar pull in silver or black, $1.00-3.00 per piece at 500 pcs MOQ, sold for kitchen and wardrobe doors on MDF and particle-board carcasses. It is the handle a cabinet workshop buys by the hundred rather than the dozen, because the same bar goes on every door of a run.

The buyers are kitchen and wardrobe fabricators in **Lagos — Alaba** and **Ogidi**, and the furniture-parts dealers in **Onitsha Main Market** who supply them. Silver is the default when the hinges and slides are nickel; black is specified when the carcass is dark or the customer is copying a showroom set. At 15-25 days the line can be reordered inside a project, and mixed containers are accepted, so the pull ships with the concealed hinges and slides for the same job in one 20ft. It is not the handle for a solid hardwood door — that load belongs to the door-handle range, not to a cabinet pull.
