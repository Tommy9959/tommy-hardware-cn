## Who buys the FF-002

FF-002 is the cam that holds a knockdown joint together: zinc alloy body with a plastic sleeve, nickel or black, priced $0.30-0.80 per set at 500 pcs MOQ. It is bought by the set against a panel count rather than by the carton on spec — a wardrobe order needs one cam per joint, so the quantity follows the furniture schedule and not a guess at the counter.

The buyers are flat-pack workshops supplying wardrobes, beds and cabinet panels in **Lagos — Alaba** and **Aba (Ariaria)**, and the furniture-parts traders in **Onitsha** who resell to those workshops. Nickel suits panels that stay visible inside a wardrobe; black disappears against dark board. Because the part is invisible once assembled, price and consistency matter more than finish: the same cam has to fit the same drill pattern on the next run, or the workshop re-bores every panel. Order the set count with the panel count and we quote against the schedule.
