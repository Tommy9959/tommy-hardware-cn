### Product Overview

BM-002 is ceramic-style wallpaper — and it's becoming one of our most popular products for Nigerian interior projects. ### What Nigerian Interior Designers Love About It

**Looks like tile, feels like wallpaper.** You get the textured, dimensional look of ceramic cladding, but it rolls out and hangs like wallpaper. Huge time and labor savings compared to actual tiling.

**Easy installation.** Any wallpaper installer can handle this. No tile cutter, no adhesive mixing, no grouting. Just paste the wall, hang the paper, trim the edges. A room that would take 2-3 days to tile can be wallpapered in a few hours.

**Durable and washable.** The ceramic coating means it's wipe-clean. Scuff marks, fingerprints, dust — all come off with a damp cloth. Much more durable than standard wallpaper.

**Multiple colors available.** We stock a range of popular colors — from neutral tones that work in any space to bolder options for feature walls.

### Installation Checklist

1. **Prepare the wall** — must be smooth, dry, and primed
2. **Apply adhesive** — standard wallpaper paste works fine
3. **Hang the paper** — butt joints, no overlap needed
4. **Smooth out bubbles** — use a wallpaper smoother
5. **Trim edges** — sharp knife + straight edge

That's it. No dust, no grout, no waiting for tiles to set.
