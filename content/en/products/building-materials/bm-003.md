|## Model BM-003 — Waterproof Coating

### Product Overview

This is one of our biggest sellers to Nigeria — and it's easy to see why. BM-003 is an acrylic polymer waterproof coating that protects roofs and walls from the heavy rains that hit most of Nigeria from April to October. If you've dealt with leaking roofs, damp walls, or water stains on ceilings, this is the solution. It's a liquid membrane that dries to form a seamless, flexible waterproof barrier.

### Why Every Nigerian Building Needs BM-003

**Rainy season protection.** Nigeria gets 1,000-3,000mm of rain annually depending on the region. That's a lot of water hitting your roofs and walls. BM-003 creates a durable waterproof layer that flexes with the building as it expands and contracts in the heat.

**Covers cracks and joints.** Old roofs develop hairline cracks. Concrete walls get shrinkage cracks. BM-003 bridges these gaps — the acrylic polymer film stretches up to 300% without tearing. No more chasing cracks every rainy season.

**Easy to apply.** Brush, roller, or spray — no special equipment needed. One person can coat a standard roof in a day. Dries in 4-6 hours, full cure in 24 hours.

**White reflects heat.** The white finish reflects sunlight, keeping the building cooler. In the Nigerian sun, that's a real bonus — lower interior temperatures mean less AC usage.

### Surface Preparation Guide

1. **Clean the surface** — remove dirt, dust, oil, loose paint
2. **Repair cracks** — fill larger cracks (>2mm) with cement mortar first
3. **Prime if needed** — porous concrete surfaces benefit from a primer coat
4. **Apply first coat** — using brush, roller, or spray
5. **Apply second coat** — after 4-6 hours, perpendicular to first coat direction
6. **Allow 24 hours cure** — before exposing to rain or foot traffic

### Where to Use BM-003

- **Flat and sloped roofs** — concrete, metal sheet, or asbestos cement
- **Exterior walls** — stop damp penetration through block walls
- **Water tanks** — interior and exterior waterproofing
- **Balconies and terraces** — prevent water seepage to floors below
- **Foundation walls** — below-ground waterproofing for basements
