|## Model BM-005 — Construction Adhesive (MS Polymer)

### Product Overview

BM-005 is an MS Polymer-based construction adhesive — and it's the product I recommend when you need something that bonds almost anything to anything. Unlike solvent-based adhesives that smell strong and can damage certain materials, MS Polymer is clean, low-odor, and incredibly strong. We ship this to Nigeria for everything from fixing baseboards to bonding wall panels to concrete.

### Why MS Polymer?

**Bonds without priming.** MS Polymer adhesive grabs onto concrete, wood, metal, ceramic, plastic, stone — almost any clean surface. No need for separate primer on most surfaces. Saves time on site.

**Flexible bond.** Buildings move. Concrete expands and contracts with temperature changes. MS Polymer stays flexible — the bond flexes instead of breaking. Perfect for Nigeria's climate where buildings heat up during the day and cool down at night.

**Weather and UV resistant.** Can be used indoors or outdoors. Won't yellow, crack, or lose strength in sunlight.

**Low odor, no solvents.** Your workers can apply it indoors without ventilation issues. No harsh chemical smell. Non-flammable — safe for storage on site.

### How to Apply

1. **Clean both surfaces** — remove dust, grease, loose material
2. **Cut nozzle** — cut at 45° angle to desired bead size
3. **Apply adhesive** — using standard caulking gun
4. **Press and hold** — 10-30 seconds depending on weight
5. **Allow to cure** — 24 hours for full strength

For heavy items, use temporary support (tape or props) while the adhesive cures.
