The Construction Adhesive (BM-005) is made of MS Polymer with a White / Gray finish, and is used for bonding panels, skirting, trims and staircase details. **Best for:** bonding panels, skirting, trims and staircase details.

### Material and finish

MS polymer adhesive bonds without isocyanate and tolerates damp surfaces better than solvent-based cartridges. It is the right choice for skirting, panels and trim on Nigerian sites where surfaces are rarely perfectly dry.
