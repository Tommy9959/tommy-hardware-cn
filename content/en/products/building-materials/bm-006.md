### Why PVC Ceiling Panels Dominate in Nigeria

**100% waterproof.** Nigeria's rainy season means high humidity indoors. Gypsum ceilings absorb moisture and sag. PVC panels? They don't care about moisture. No sagging, no staining, no mold. Wipe them down with a cloth and they look new again.

**No painting needed.** The color goes all the way through. White stays white. Wood grain stays wood grain. No repainting every few years like with plasterboard ceilings.

**Easy installation.** One man can install 20-30 m² of PVC ceiling panels per day. Aluminum T-grid system goes up fast, panels click into place. No plastering, no sanding, no dust.

**Lightweight.** A full PVC ceiling weighs a fraction of a plasterboard ceiling. Less load on the roof structure. Important in Nigerian buildings where roof framing might be minimal.

### Installation Overview

1. **Fix battens or T-grid** — spaced at 40-50cm centers, level across the room
2. **Cut panels to length** — using a sharp knife or fine-tooth saw
3. **Start from one side** — click first panel into the channel
4. **Continue across** — each panel locks into the previous one
5. **Finish with trim** — edge trim around the perimeter for a clean look

A 4m x 5m room (20 m²) takes about one day with one experienced installer.
