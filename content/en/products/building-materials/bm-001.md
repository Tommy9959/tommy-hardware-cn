### Application Tips (From Real Job Sites)

1. **Surface prep matters.** Make sure the wall or floor is clean, free of dust, and dampened before applying. Dry surfaces suck the moisture out of the adhesive too fast.
2. **Use the right trowel.** For floor tiles, use a 10mm notched trowel. For wall tiles, 6mm is enough.
3. **Don't mix too much at once.** Mix only what you can use in 20-30 minutes. Once it starts setting, don't add more water — throw it away and mix fresh.
4. **Back-buttering.** For large-format tiles (60x60cm and above), apply a thin layer on the tile back too. This prevents hollow spots.
