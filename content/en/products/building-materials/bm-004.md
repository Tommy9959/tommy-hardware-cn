BM-004 is wall putty powder — and if you're doing interior finishing in Nigeria, this is an essential product. Before you paint any wall, you need a smooth surface. That's what this putty does. **Fills the gaps.** New block walls always have minor depressions, pinholes, and uneven spots. Paint doesn't hide these — it highlights them. A coat of wall putty before painting transforms rough walls into smooth, paintable surfaces.

**One product, multiple uses.** Use it on:
- Concrete walls
- Block/brick walls
- Plastered surfaces
- Ceilings
- Repair patches

**Low shrinkage.** Our polymer-modified formulation shrinks less than standard putty. That means fewer cracks after drying, less rework.

**Sands easily.** Once dry (24 hours), BM-004 sands smooth with basic sandpaper. No need for power sanders — hand sanding works fine.

### Application Guide

**Step 1: Prepare the wall.** Clean off dust, oil, and loose particles. Dampen the surface slightly — this helps adhesion.

**Step 2: Mix.** Add clean water to the powder (ratio ~1:3 water to powder by volume). Mix until smooth, no lumps. Let it sit for 5 minutes, then mix again.

**Step 3: Apply.** Use a steel trowel or putty knife. Apply first coat at 1-2mm thickness. Focus on filling depressions and covering rough areas.

**Step 4: Let dry.** 4-6 hours between coats. Apply second coat if needed for a smoother finish.

**Step 5: Sand.** After 24 hours, sand lightly with 120-180 grit paper. Dust off, then prime and paint.
