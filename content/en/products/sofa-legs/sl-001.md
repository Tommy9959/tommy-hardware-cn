## Who buys the SL-001

The SL-001 is the wood leg of the range: solid beech in natural, walnut or black, $2.00-6.00 a piece at a 500-piece MOQ. It exists for furniture where the leg is part of the design — **upholstered sofas and classic furniture where wood is visible** — so the finish is a furniture decision, made against the frame and the fabric, not a hardware one.

The buyers are upholstery workshops and classic-furniture makers: the sofa shops in **Aba (Ariaria)** and **Onitsha** building three-piece suites, the furniture traders in **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** who stock replacement legs, and joinery shops in **Ogidi** matching a leg to a client's timber. Walnut is the seller for stained frames, natural for light wood, black for painted or very dark pieces.

Where it is the wrong choice: a bathroom unit, an outdoor sofa or any floor that stays wet — beech is an interior leg, and the plastic-footed SL-003 is the better answer where water sits. Order the colour split with the carton count.
