## Who buys the SL-005

The SL-005 is the decorative foot: zinc alloy in antique brass or chrome, $2.00-6.00 a piece, MOQ 500 pieces. It is the premium end of this range — the finish is meant to be seen, and antique brass is what a buyer reaches for when the sofa or cabinet is being sold as a classic piece rather than a utility one.

The stated applications are **premium upholstery, hotel furniture and decorative cabinets**: contract upholsterers fitting out hotel rooms in **Abuja** and **Port Harcourt**, the higher-end sofa workshops in **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo**, and cabinet makers in **Onitsha and Ogidi** doing reception and display pieces. Zinc alloy takes a decorative casting in a way a plain steel leg does not, which is why shape is the selling point here rather than load.

Where it is the wrong choice: heavy commercial seating, where the solid steel SL-006 belongs, and any wet floor unless the base is protected. Antique brass and chrome are two different customers — keep the split on the order.
