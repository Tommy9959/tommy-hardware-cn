## Who buys the SL-002

The SL-002 is the two-finish leg: steel in chrome or matte black, $2.00-6.00 a piece, MOQ 500 pieces. Holding both finishes under one model number is the point — a workshop building a run of modern sofas keeps one leg on the shelf and changes the look on the order, instead of carrying two SKUs.

The applications are **modern sofas, TV consoles and low cabinets**, which is how it goes out: sofa workshops in **Aba (Ariaria)** and **Onitsha**, TV-console makers and furniture traders in **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo**, and shop fitters in **Ogidi** using it under low display cabinets. Chrome sells to the showroom buyer; matte black goes with black powder-coated frames and is the finish that hides a scuff.

Where it is the wrong choice: heavy sectional frames, where the solid steel SL-006 belongs, and wet floors, where the plated face marks unless the base is protected. Give us the chrome/black split with the order so the cartons come packed the way the workshop uses them.
