## Who buys the SL-006

The SL-006 is the only solid steel leg in this range: powder coated, $2.00-6.00 a piece, MOQ 500 pieces. It is the one you specify when the frame is heavy and the leg is not the part you want to think about again — sectional seating, commercial furniture and heavy frames, in the sheet's own wording.

The buyers are commercial furniture workshops and contract upholsterers: hotel and reception seating in **Abuja** and **Port Harcourt**, sectional sofa builders in **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** supplying the showrooms, and the upholstery shops in **Aba (Ariaria)** that take repeat orders from offices. Powder coating is deliberate here — it takes the scuffs of a leg that gets kicked, where a plated face would show every knock.
