The HH-005 is what I recommend when security matters. This isn't your standard butt hinge — it's beefed up for doors that need to stay put. ### Security-First Design

**Thicker leaf gauge.** The HH-005 uses 3.0mm thick material compared to the standard 2.5mm. That extra half millimeter makes a real difference in preventing door sag and resisting forced entry.

**Non-removable pin design.** Once installed, the pin cannot be tapped out from the bottom. This is critical for outward-swinging doors commonly found in Nigerian commercial properties. A standard hinge can be compromised in seconds by someone with a hammer and screwdriver. HH-005 prevents that.

**Heavy weight rating.** Handles doors up to 100kg+ — solid core, metal-clad, fire-rated. Whatever heavy door your project requires, this hinge carries it.
