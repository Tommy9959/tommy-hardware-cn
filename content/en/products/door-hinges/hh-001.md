## Who buys the HH-001

HH-001 is a door hinge in stainless steel, steel or brass, satin, polished or zinc plated, at $1.50-4.00 per piece with an MOQ of 100 pcs, 200-400 g per pair, rated on the page for door leaves up to 80 kg. It is a volume line: hinges ship by the carton and are counted in pairs per door, so the order is always a multiple of the door count on the job.

Buyers are gate fabricators, door assemblers and hardware wholesalers. **Lagos — Alaba** shops pair it with locks and handles in the same container; **Onitsha Main Market** and **Ogidi** dealers restock it against the steel door trade; **Port Harcourt** fabricators take the zinc plated option for painted steel doors, while **Abuja** project suppliers specify satin for interior doors.

Finish is the only real decision at this price: zinc plated for painted steel doors, satin or polished where the hinge is visible. Coastal sites need the plating checked before the carton count is fixed, because salt air is unkind to a thin coating.
