## Who buys the HH-002

HH-002 carries the same hinge specification as the rest of the range — stainless steel, steel or brass, satin, polished or zinc plated, $1.50-4.00 per piece, MOQ 100 pcs, 200-400 g per pair, door leaves up to 80 kg — so the order is decided by market and plating, not by a spec difference.

The buyer is the door assembler rather than the lock trader: someone hanging door leaves in **Aba (Ariaria)** and **Port Harcourt**, where hinges are bought in pairs to a door count and the finish has to match a batch of painted steel doors. **Onitsha** and **Ogidi** wholesalers take it alongside the HH-001 so the rack shows two patterns at one price. **Lagos — Alaba** shops use it as the pair-line for handles and closers in a mixed container.

Practical note: order hinges by pair to the door schedule, not by carton guess, and confirm zinc plated versus satin before deposit — the two look different on a finished door even though the price band is the same.
