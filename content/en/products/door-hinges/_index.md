### Which hinge sizes actually move in Nigeria

- **4"x3" butt hinge** — the volume seller. Standard residential doors, sold by the pair or by the box in every building-materials market.
- **5"x4" butt hinge** — heavier exterior and security doors; buyers fitting steel doors ask for this size.
- **3"x3"** — cabinet, wardrobe and light interior doors.
- **5"x5" and 4"x4"** — commercial doors and gate leaves where the leaf is heavier than a standard panel.

If you are buying for a door factory rather than a retail shelf, tell us the leaf weight and frame thickness — we will match the hinge instead of letting you over-specify and lose margin.

### Material choice for Nigeria's climate

Nigeria is two different markets for hinges:

- **Coastal (Lagos, Port Harcourt, Warri, Calabar):** humidity and salt air attack zinc-plated steel fast. SS304 is the safe specification and what serious importers stock for the south.
- **Inland (Kano, Kaduna, Abuja, Jos):** drier air lets zinc-plated cold rolled steel hold up well and protects your price point.

Cheap uncoated iron looks fine in the shop and rusts on the door. That is the most common complaint we hear from Nigerian traders, and it is why we quote SS304 as the default for coastal buyers.
