## Who buys the DA-002

The DA-002 is an automatic door closer in zinc alloy and steel, silver or white, at $5.00-12.00 per pc with an MOQ of 500 pcs. It is the one item on this page that is specified by the building rather than by the counter: a door that has to close itself behind people is a corridor, office or fire-door requirement, and the buyer needs the closing action, not a pretty finish.

Buyers are **Abuja** and **Lagos** project suppliers fitting out offices and flats, plus **Port Harcourt** contractors on commercial buildings where the door schedule already calls for a self-closer. **Onitsha** and **Ogidi** wholesalers keep a small quantity for the shop trade, but the volume order comes from the project side, quoted together with hinges and locks for the same door.

Two things to settle before deposit: the door weight the closer has to pull, and the finish — silver or white — because the two are not interchangeable on a finished corridor. The 500 pcs MOQ is per model, so a mixed container with hinges, handles and stoppers is normal.
