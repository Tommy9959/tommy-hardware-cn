## Who buys the DA-006

The DA-006 finger guard is an aluminium and rubber strip for the hinge side of a door, silver or gray, at $1.00-3.50 per pc with an MOQ of 500 pcs. The buyer is whoever has been told the door is a hazard: a school, a clinic, a nursery, or a landlord fitting out flats where children live.

In practice that means **Abuja** and **Lagos** project suppliers on institutional and residential fit-out, and **Port Harcourt** contractors on office and clinic work. **Onitsha** and **Ogidi** dealers take it as a small add-on, because the customer who has just bought a door and hinges will take a finger guard in the same visit — a 500 pcs carton that sells by the piece off the counter.

It is not a structural part. It does not fix a door that already slams, and it does not replace a door closer. Sell it where the hinge gap is exposed and children or heavy foot traffic use the door: that is the one place the aluminium and rubber strip earns its price.
