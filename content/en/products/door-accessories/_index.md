### The accessories that actually move

- **Automatic door closers (DA-002).** The highest-value item in this category and the one most often bought wrong. Size 2-3 for standard residential doors up to 60 kg, size 4 for metal security doors. Always ask for the closing-speed and latching-speed adjustment; the cheapest closers have neither and slam.
- **Door viewers (DA-003).** Brass body, 200-degree lens, fits 35-55 mm doors. Every apartment door in Lagos and Abuja is a potential sale, and the metal security door market is bigger than it looks.
- **Rubber door stoppers (DA-001).** High-frequency, low-price item that keeps small customers returning. Steel-core versions survive longer than all-rubber ones.
- **Slide bolts (DA-005).** Stainless steel for exterior and bathroom doors. This is a rust-sensitive item in coastal cities — chrome zinc alloy looks the same in the catalogue and fails in a year.
- **Security chains (DA-004).** Sold together with viewers as an apartment entry kit; bundle them for a better average order.
- **Finger guards and angle brackets (DA-006, DA-007).** The add-on lines that fill cartons and lift container utilisation.

### Sizing and material traps

1. **Door weight, not door width.** Closers are selected by door weight and width together; a wide lightweight door still needs a size 3 spring. Ask the buyer for the door spec before quoting.
2. **Plating.** Chrome over zinc alloy is fine indoors. On an exterior gate in Port Harcourt it will pit within a season — sell stainless there.
3. **Door thickness.** Viewers come in 35-55 mm and 45-75 mm versions. Stocking only one leaves half the market unserved.
