## Who buys the 9379-183

The 9379-183 is a one-design buy. The 183 pattern appears once in the iron plate catalogue, so it does not compete with the 113, 151 or 231 handles for shelf space — it competes on being the same lock on every door of a block. That is how it is ordered around **Ogidi and greater Onitsha**, where a supplier fits out a row of units and has to hand over doors that match.

The order is 1000 sets, 20 sets per carton at 2.0-3.5 kg per set, 15-25 days of bulk after a 5-7 day sample. Mixed containers are accepted for the retail part of the same shop, but the project quantity is what makes the model worth stocking.

One thing to settle early: the finish. With a single design going onto a whole site, a finish the buyer did not expect is a much bigger problem than it is on a mixed shelf — and on the coast the plating question has to be answered before production, not after.
