## Who buys the 755-151

The 755-151 is the entry point into the iron plate range: a straight iron plate body with an aluminium handle in AB antique bronze, shipped in export cartons of 24 pcs at 24.6 kg. That combination is why it moves fastest in **Onitsha Main Market and Ogidi**, where traders restock a handful of designs in multiples of 400 and let the handle design do the selling on the counter.

In **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** — the same lock is bought as part of a mixed order, usually alongside the 837 and 955 series, because shop customers there ask to compare handles rather than accept one pattern. In **Aba (Ariaria)** it is the price-led SKU on the shelf.
