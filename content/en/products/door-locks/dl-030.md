755A-151 differs from the 755A-131 in handle pattern and is ordered on the same terms: $2.50-5.00 a set, 100 sets minimum, 2.0-3.5 kg per set in an individual box.

Shops that carry both do it deliberately — two patterns at one price let a counter customer choose without the shop carrying two price points. That is the usual arrangement in **Onitsha Main Market** and **Ogidi**, where the boxed 755A pair sits beside the 24-per-carton 755-113 for buyers who want volume rather than boxes.

In **Aba (Ariaria)** it is the second pattern a stall adds once the first one proves.
