### Which lock for which door

- **Steel security doors (the volume market):** 755, 837, 955 and 905 series in AB antique bronze. These are what building-materials traders in Onitsha, Ogidi, Alaba and Ladipo restock.
- **Wooden doors:** the same mortise body, but check the plate width against the door stile before ordering — a 24 pcs/carton model is heavier for a reason.
- **Interior and office doors:** lighter models with lower carton weight, usually ordered in mixed cartons to keep freight per unit down.

If you tell us the door type, leaf thickness and the finish your market already sells, we quote the matching models instead of letting you guess from a price list.
