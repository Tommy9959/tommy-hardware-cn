## Who buys the 905-43

The 905-43 is the trial SKU of the 905 range: the 43 code is short, it is not one of the handle patterns already sitting on most Nigerian counters, and it sells at the same $3.00-5.00 per piece as the rest of the series. That makes it the model a trader adds when he wants to see whether a new pattern moves before he gives it container space.

The buyers are lock traders in **Aba — Ariaria** and smaller wholesalers around Onitsha who order one design at a time and watch the counter for a month before reordering. MOQ is 1000 sets, packed 20 sets per carton at 2.0-3.5 kg per set, bulk in 15-25 days after a 5-7 day sample.

It is a test, so treat it that way: one finish, one carton count, no assumption that it repeats. If the 43 pattern sells, the same container can be refilled with the 905-151 and 905-352, which are the safer volumes.
