## Who buys the ST-005

The ST-005 is the glass line of this range: stainless steel and brass track in brushed or polished finish, $5.00-12.00 a meter, MOQ 500 meters. It is bought with the panel, not before it, because the track has to match the frame the glazier is fitting.

The buyers are shopfitters and glass fabricators doing **shop display units and bathroom glass partitions**: showroom cabinets in **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** and **Onitsha**, and shower or partition screens in hotel and residential bathroom work. Stainless steel with brass is why it is quoted for the wet side — a bathroom partition sees water daily, and the brushed finish hides the handling marks that polished shows on a shopfront. In **Port Harcourt** the same track goes into pharmacy and clinic display units.

Where it is the wrong choice: a heavy timber barn door, which needs the ST-006, or a wardrobe run, where the aluminium ST-002 is the lighter meter. Tell us the panel thickness when you order so the meters are fixed against the right frame.
