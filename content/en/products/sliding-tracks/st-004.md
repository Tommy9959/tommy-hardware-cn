The ST-004 is the only damper model in the sliding range: steel slide with a damper, zinc plated, $5.00-12.00 a meter, MOQ 500 meters. The damper is the part a customer actually notices — the drawer pulls itself shut over the last few centimetres instead of banging — so it is specified by people selling a kitchen, not by people selling a drawer.

Those buyers are kitchen and wardrobe joinery shops working on **mid-market kitchen drawers that must close quietly**: cabinet workshops in **Ogidi and Onitsha**, the furniture-parts traders in **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** who supply them, and the upholstery and joinery shops in **Aba (Ariaria)** quoting rented flats and small hotels. Zinc plating is a drawer-part finish — nobody sees it once the drawer is in the carcass.
