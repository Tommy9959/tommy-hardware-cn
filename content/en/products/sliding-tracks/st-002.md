## Who buys the ST-002

The ST-002 is the aluminium line: anodized silver rail at $5.00-12.00 a meter, MOQ 500 meters, for sliding wardrobe doors and room dividers. Aluminium is why it is the wardrobe default — it stays light on a top-hung door, and there is no plated steel face to mark where hands go.

The buyers are wardrobe and cabinet workshops in **Ogidi and Onitsha**, plus the furniture-parts traders in **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** who supply them. A room divider in a rented flat is the other steady job: one rail, two panels, and the anodized silver has to sit with whatever the carpenter used on the frame. In **Aba (Ariaria)** it is bought by the meter for shop fitting-out.

Where it is the wrong choice: a heavy barn door or a workshop panel, which needs the steel ST-006 or ST-003, and a glass shower screen, which is the ST-005. This is a per-meter line, so measure the opening before you order — the meters, not the cartons, set the price.
