The Wardrobe Sliding Rail (ST-002) is made of Aluminum Alloy with a Anodized Silver finish, and is used for sliding wardrobe doors and room dividers. It is one of the steady-volume models in our sliding tracks range: the mould is always in production, so repeat orders ship faster than new ones.

### Material and finish

Aluminium alloy is light, does not rust, and finishes cleanly in anodised silver or powder-coated black. It is the right choice where weight matters — wardrobe rails, pull handles and furniture legs — but bare polished aluminium dulls in humid air, so anodising is not optional.
