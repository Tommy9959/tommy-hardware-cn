## Who buys the ST-006

The ST-006 is the barn-door line: steel track in matte black or rustic, priced by the meter at $5.00-12.00, MOQ 500 meters. It is a fit-out part rather than a stock item — the buyer measures the opening, orders meters, and the finish has to sit beside timber rather than against it.

That puts it with joinery shops and fit-out contractors working on **barn-style timber doors in residential fit-outs**: interior doors in new-build houses, café and office partitions, and the sliding timber door on a wardrobe run. In **Ogidi and Onitsha** the cabinet workshops buy it beside hinges and handles so one delivery fits the whole job; in **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** traders hold the matte black as the default and a few meters of rustic.

Where it is the wrong choice: a glass panel or a shop display unit — that is the ST-005, glass door track in stainless steel and brass. And this is a per-meter line, so meters, not cartons, are what you quote the customer.
