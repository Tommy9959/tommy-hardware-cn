## Who buys the ST-001

The ST-001 is a full-extension slide in cold rolled steel, zinc plated, quoted $5.00–12.00 a metre with a 500 metres MOQ and a 15–20 day production run. Full extension is the specification that matters: the drawer comes all the way out, which is what a kitchen fitter needs for a pot drawer and a wardrobe fitter needs for a deep shelf.

The buyers are furniture-parts wholesalers and cabinet makers. **Aba (Ariaria)** is the strongest of them — the furniture cluster there buys slides and hinges together so a kitchen run can be fitted from one delivery; **Onitsha Main Market and Ogidi** traders take slides as a mixed line beside handles and legs; **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** depots hold the metre stock for fitters. Orders are worked out in metres and converted to drawer count, not guessed by carton.

Where it is the wrong choice: heavy commercial drawers beyond the rated load, or drawers deeper than 500mm — ask for the load rating and the next size up before quoting.
