## Who buys the ST-003

The ST-003 is the heavy panel line: steel track with nylon rollers, powder coated, $5.00-12.00 a meter at a 500-meter MOQ. The nylon roller is what separates it from the rest of the range — it runs quieter and takes the wear that a steel roller would put into the track on a door opened all day.

That is the **workshop doors, shop fronts and heavy sliding panels** job: fabrication shops and warehouses in **Port Harcourt and Onne**, lock-up shop fronts in **Aba (Ariaria)** and **Onitsha**, and sliding security panels on commercial buildings. Powder coating is the finish the buyer sees, and on a shopfront it is also the surface that takes the dust and the sun.

Where it is the wrong choice: a wardrobe door or a room divider, where the aluminium ST-002 is lighter and cheaper by the meter, or a glass display unit, which belongs to the ST-005. On the coast, say so when you order — we would rather quote the right finish than have a plated one go into salt air.
