## Who buys the HHL

HHL is the heavy rectangle body in the padlock range: three sizes at 74, 84 and 94 mm, all on a 12.8 mm shackle, $2.40-2.71 per piece at 50 pcs MOQ, 24 pcs to the carton. The 84 and 94 mm bodies are listed at 956 g and 1050 g, so the weight alone tells a buyer this is not the line for a luggage lock.

The buyers are security-goods distributors and hardware shops in **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo**, and the gate and shutter trade around **Onitsha Main Market**, who quote the size by what is being locked: a shop shutter, a container door, a compound gate. The solid brass cylinder and A3 steel shackle are what the customer checks before the price. Because cartons are packed one size at 24 pcs, a mixed order is quoted as separate carton lines, and the 50 pcs MOQ lets a shop try one size first. For salt-air sites the plated finish on this line is the wrong choice — the waterproof plastic-cover series in this range is the one to quote.
