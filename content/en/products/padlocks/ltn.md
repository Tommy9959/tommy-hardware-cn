This model is specified for gates, shop shutters, containers and lockers — typically outdoor and industrial use.  Check the finish against the door it will sit on: Imitate Brass Painting is what the buyer sees first, and it decides repeat orders more than the price line does.

## Who buys the LTN

The LTN is the brass-look line: iron body, solid brass cylinder, A3 steel shackle, finished in imitate brass painting, in six sizes from 30mm to 80mm at $0.64–1.81 and 48–144 pcs to the carton. It exists because a lot of counter customers want the brass colour and will not pay for a solid brass body — so it is bought as a visible upgrade at a plain padlock price.

The buyers are market-stall and shop-counter traders rather than industrial accounts. **Onitsha Main Market and Ogidi** stalls sell the 30mm, 40mm and 50mm sizes straight off the card to shutter and locker buyers; **Aba (Ariaria)** traders take the same small sizes in carton quantities; **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** distributors carry the full six-size run and split it by shop. 60mm to 80mm go to gate buyers who want the look without the weight of the heavy series.

Where it is the wrong choice: salt-air sites. The painted finish is the weaker option on the coast — the waterproof plastic-cover series is the right answer there.
