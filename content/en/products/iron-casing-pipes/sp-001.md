It is the size an electrical contractor embeds in a slab or block wall to pull cable through, not the size a fabricator uses to carry load.

The buyers are electrical and building-services contractors on housing and shop developments around **Onitsha** and **Ogidi**, and the building-material traders in **Onitsha Main Market** who stock it beside cement and conduit boxes. Galvanized is ordered where the pipe sits in damp blockwork or an exposed run; black is the cheaper choice for a fully embedded slab.
