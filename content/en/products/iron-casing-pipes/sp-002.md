The buyers are steel-work fabricators and fence and gate contractors working out of **Aba (Ariaria)** and **Onitsha**, plus building-material traders in **Ogidi** supplying site work. Galvanized is taken for anything that stays exposed or sits in wet ground; black is ordered where the pipe is cast in and never seen again.
