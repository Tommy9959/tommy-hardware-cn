## Who buys the IP-005

The SP-005 is the heavy end of the 25mm casing group: same 25mm bore and 3m or 6m lengths as the lighter pipes, but a 2.5mm wall, $2.00–3.00 a metre and a 500 pcs MOQ instead of 100. The thicker wall and the higher minimum both point at the same buyer — someone pouring foundations or building a load-bearing frame, not someone pulling cable through a wall.

That is why the orders come from steel-work fabricators and foundation contractors rather than shop traders. **Port Harcourt and Onne** take it for industrial and residential foundation work, and **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** depots hold it for fabricators who buy bundles against a project. **Onitsha Main Market and Ogidi** traders usually pass on this one and stock the lighter pipe instead.

Where it is the wrong choice: conduit runs, where the 1.5mm SP-003 does the job for less, and any use that requires a certified structural section.
