## Who buys the IP-003

The SP-003 is the general-purpose member of the 25mm casing group: 1.5mm wall, 3m or 6m lengths, $1.20–2.00 a metre and only 100 pcs MOQ — the lowest entry point of the 25mm sizes. That low minimum is why it is the pipe a first-time conduit buyer tests with, and why it also works as a top-up line for a contractor who has run short mid-pour.

The buyers are electrical contractors and building-material traders. In **Port Harcourt and Onne** the site demand is steady because industrial and residential work runs at the same time; **Onitsha Main Market and Ogidi** traders keep bundles for resale to small contractors; **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** depots stock it beside fittings and boxes.
