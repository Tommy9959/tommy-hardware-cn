## Who buys the DH-002

The DH-002 is an aluminium alloy 6063 lever handle, anodized or powder coated, at $1.00-2.50 per piece with an MOQ of 500 pcs, 320 g per set, a 60 mm backset and a 35-45 mm door thickness range. That last number decides the order: it fits standard interior and office doors, not the heavy timber leaves used on some residential entrance sets.

Buyers are **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** handle traders who buy by design and restock by pattern; **Abuja** and **Kano** shops supplying office fit-outs, where a light aluminium lever is enough because the door is not a security door; **Onitsha** dealers taking it as the mid-price option between the value zinc handles and the SS304 lever.

Where it is the wrong choice: an exterior or gate door. Aluminium 6063 in an anodized or powder-coated finish is built for interior and office use; for coastal or heavy-traffic doors go to the SS304 lever instead. Before the deposit, confirm door thickness and backset from site: 35-45 mm and 60 mm respectively.
