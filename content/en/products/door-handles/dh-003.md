## Who buys the DH-003

The DH-003 is the low-MOQ option in the handle range: 50 pcs, $2.50–5.00 per set, 400–500 g a set, built for door leaves of 35–50mm and offered in zinc alloy, brass or stainless steel with a customisable finish. That 50-piece minimum is the whole point — it lets a trader test a pattern without sinking a container into it.

So the buyers are handle traders and door fitters who sell by design. In **Kano and Abuja** the showroom trade buys one pattern at a time and reorders the one that moves; in **Onitsha Main Market and Ogidi** the same trader keeps four or five lever shapes on the wall and mixes them inside one carton. **Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo** shops treat it as the upgrade line above the plain lever.

Where it is the wrong choice: budget projects that compare on price alone, where a lighter lever will undercut this one, and doors outside the 35–50mm range.
