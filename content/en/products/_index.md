## How to choose a category

- **Building or fitting doors?** Locks, hinges and handles are the three that decide whether a door works. Start with the lock, because the door is cut for it.
- **Supplying furniture workshops?** Cabinet hardware, drawer slides and furniture fittings move together; sofa legs come from the same buyers.
- **Trading in a market?** Padlocks and hinges turn over fastest, locks carry the highest value per carton.
- **Coastal city?** Specify SS304 hinges and plated lock faces. The 20-80 cent difference per piece is cheaper than replacing hardware in a year.

## What every order includes

- **FOB or CIF pricing** with carton dimensions and gross weight, so freight can be calculated before you commit.
- **Export packing**: locks at 20 sets per carton, hinges and fittings by weight, all cartons stencilled with your shipping mark.
- **Documents**: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, plus SONCAP, Form M and PAAR support at no extra fee.
- **Mixed containers**: any combination of the categories above can share one 20ft or 40HQ.
