Choosing the right leg for the job

  • Solid beech wood (SL-001). The classic look for upholstered sofas sold in Lagos showrooms. Must be kiln-dried to under 12% moisture or it splits in the dry season — we only ship kiln-dried stock, lacquered in natural, walnut or black.
  • Steel (SL-002, SL-006). Chrome or matte black taper legs for modern sofas, and heavy-duty powder-coated legs for sectional seating and commercial furniture. Load rating is the reason to pay more here.
  • Aluminium (SL-004). Light, corrosion resistant, and the right choice for cabinets, TV consoles and bathroom vanity units.
  • Zinc alloy (SL-005). Decorative furniture feet in antique brass or chrome for premium upholstery and hotel furniture.
  • Adjustable plastic with steel insert (SL-003). Budget line, but the steel insert is what stops the leg shearing at the mounting plate. Do not stock the all-plastic version if your customers assemble on site.

Height, thread and mounting details that cause returns

Three specifications decide whether a reorder happens:

  1. Height. 100 mm and 120 mm dominate Nigerian sofa production; 150 mm is the modern “floating sofa” look. Confirm the finished seat height the buyer wants, not just the leg height.
  2. Thread. M8 is standard for wooden legs with a hanger bolt; M10 for heavy frames. Mixing these at the assembly line is the most common complaint we see.
  3. Plate and screws. Universal 4-hole plate, 38 mm and 50 mm plate sizes. We include matching screws; tell us the frame thickness.
