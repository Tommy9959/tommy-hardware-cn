What sells, and what does not

  • Tile adhesive (BM-001). Steady demand wherever tiles are laid, but buyers are price-anchored against local cement-mix practice. The sellable argument is coverage per bag and bond strength on large-format tiles, not the bag price alone.
  • Wall putty (BM-004). Sells to painters and finishing contractors. White, fine-ground putty that sands easily wins; gritty putty kills the repeat order.
  • Waterproof coating (BM-003). Roofs, bathrooms, water tanks. Two-coat acrylic systems are the standard specification in Nigerian residential builds.
  • PVC ceiling panels (BM-006). Fast-moving in the south-east and south-south where PVC ceilings are standard in new builds. Panel width and tongue profile must match local trims — confirm before loading.
  • Construction adhesive (BM-005). MS polymer cartridges for panel fixing, skirting and staircase trim. Sold alongside putty and sealant.
  • Ceramic wallpaper (BM-002). Design-led, slower moving, best as a filler line rather than a container driver.

Storage, climate and handling

Nigerian humidity destroys dry goods faster than anything else in this category:

  • Keep bags on pallets, never on concrete floors, and never store against an external wall.
  • Ship in lined or moisture-barrier bags; ask for a desiccant sachet in each carton for cartridges.
  • Cartridges and coatings have a temperature ceiling — do not ship them in the same container block as goods that need ventilation.
  • Sell through within six months; print the production date on every bag and train your resellers to rotate stock.

Container planning and mixed loads

  • Chemicals and dry powders travel on pallets at the door end of the container; hardware goes behind them.
  • A 20GP typically holds 18-20 tonnes — weight, not volume, is the limit for powders.
  • MOQ: 500 bags/rolls/tubes per model, 500 m² for panels.
  • Lead time: 15-25 days after deposit, plus 30-45 days sea freight to Lagos.

Shipping, SONCAP and payment

  • Terms: FOB Ningbo, CIF Lagos (Apapa, Tin Can), Onne, Port Harcourt.
  • Documents: Form M, SONCAP where applicable, PAAR, MSDS for chemicals, and Bill of Lading — prepared by us.
  • Payment: 30% T/T deposit, 70% before shipment; LC at sight over USD 20,000.
