Where it is used

This model is specified for entrance doors where the handle is the first thing a buyer touches — typically residential, hotel and office doors. Application on site: Interior/Exterior doors. Check the finish against the door or cabinet it will sit on: Satin / Chrome / Polished is what the buyer sees first, and it decides repeat orders more than the price line does.

Who buys the DH-001

The DH-001 is an SS304 stainless steel lever handle in satin, chrome or polished finish, at $3.50-5.00 per piece with an MOQ of 50 pcs, 450 g per set, a 60 mm or 70 mm backset and a 35-50 mm door thickness range. The stainless body is why it is specified where a handle has to survive humidity and daily use: exterior doors, hotel corridors, office entrances.

Buyers are Port Harcourt and Onne hardware dealers, where salt air dulls plated finishes faster than inland buyers expect; Lagos — Alaba and Ladipo traders selling to hotels and offices; Abuja project suppliers on commercial fit-out. In Onitsha it sits at the top of the handle rack, sold against the aluminium and zinc models rather than instead of them.

The 50 pcs MOQ is the lowest in this range, so it is also the sensible first order: take one finish and one backset, and see what the counter does with it. Satin for corridors and offices, polished for show, chrome where it must match a chrome lock on the same door.

FAQ

How much does DH-001 cost? $3.50 - $5.00 USD / piece FOB Yiwu, depending on quantity and finish. Freight to Lagos is quoted separately.

What is the MOQ for DH-001? 50 pcs. Mixed containers are accepted, so this model can share a 20ft or 40HQ with other items.

Which doors does DH-001 fit? Interior/Exterior doors. Tell us the door type and leaf or panel size and we confirm the model before you pay a deposit.

What finish is available for DH-001? Satin / Chrome / Polished. Other finishes can be produced on request; finishes are quoted per order because they change the unit price.

Why choose the DH-001 in SS304 rather than a plated handle? Because the body is stainless, not a plated base metal, so humidity and daily handling do not wear through a coating. That is why it is the handle for coastal sites, hotel corridors and exterior doors in Port Harcourt and Onne.
