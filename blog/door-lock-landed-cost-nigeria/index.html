Landed Cost: What a $3 Door Lock Really Costs in Lagos (2026)

Quick answer

A door lock quoted at $3.00 FOB Yiwu lands in Lagos at roughly $4.20-4.40 per piece once ocean freight, import duty (about 15% of CIF), 7.5% VAT, clearing and terminal charges are added. Lagos wholesalers typically resell at $5.50-6.50 and retailers at $8-12. That gap — not the factory price — is where most of the buyer's money goes.

The short version

A door lock quoted at $3.00 FOB Yiwu does not cost $3.00 in Lagos. Add freight, duty, VAT, clearing and the importer’s margin and you get:

Stage Per piece Notes
FOB Yiwu $3.00 factory price, seller’s port
Ocean freight +$0.20 20ft container, ~15,000-20,000 pcs
Import duty +$0.50 roughly 15% of CIF value
VAT 7.5% +$0.28 on CIF + duty
Clearing, terminal, PAAR +$0.20 agent fees, port charges, trucking
Landed cost ≈$4.20-4.40 before the importer’s margin
Lagos wholesale $5.50-6.50 importer → distributor
Lagos retail $8-12 shop price to a household

The factory price is roughly 70% of what the buyer eventually pays. That is normal — nobody moves a container for free — but it explains why “I found it cheaper on Alibaba” rarely survives contact with the port.

Where the money actually goes

Freight is the smallest line. At 15,000-20,000 locks per 20ft container, ocean freight is a few cents to twenty cents per piece. Buying one carton by air will cost more in freight than the goods.

Duty and VAT are the big lines. Duty depends on the HS code assigned to the product (locks, hinges and handles sit in different headings) and the tariff in force when the Form M is processed. VAT is charged on the CIF value plus duty, so it compounds.

Clearing is the line nobody budgets for. Terminal handling, shipping line charges, agent commission, PAAR processing and trucking out of Apapa or Tin Can typically add 3-6% of cargo value. Demurrage, when documents are late, can exceed everything else in a single week.

Rates change. Treat the numbers above as an order of magnitude and confirm the current tariff and clearing fees with your agent before you price a container.

What this means when you negotiate

  1. Compare landed cost, not FOB. A supplier $0.20 cheaper per piece but two weeks slower can lose you more in demurrage and lost sales than the discount is worth.
  2. Ask for the CIF price. If a supplier quotes CIF Lagos, the freight is already inside and the comparison is honest.
  3. Volume decides the channel. Below a few thousand pieces, buy through a Lagos wholesaler or a consolidator. Above that, a direct container starts to pay for itself.
  4. Documents are part of the price. A supplier who cannot support SONCAP, Form M and PAAR is cheaper on paper and more expensive at the port.

The same maths on the other categories

The pattern repeats across the catalogue:

  • Hinges at $0.50 FOB land around $0.70-0.80 and wholesale at $1.00-1.20.
  • Padlocks at $0.42-2.71 FOB land 35-45% higher.
  • Door handles at $1.50-3.00 FOB land around $2.10-2.60 and wholesale at $3.00-4.00.

Use the same structure — freight per piece, duty, VAT, clearing, margin — and the estimate holds within 10-15%.

Questions buyers ask

Is it cheaper to import from China myself? Above roughly 10,000 pieces per order, yes. Below that, consolidation or a Lagos wholesaler usually wins once you count clearing time and demurrage risk.

Why do two suppliers quote very different CIF prices? Different ports of loading, different container utilisation and sometimes different product weight. A quote that looks 15% cheaper often means thinner plating or a lighter carton.

Can I see the landed cost before I commit? Ask for CIF Lagos and a packing list with carton dimensions and gross weight — those three numbers are enough to estimate the rest.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total landed cost of a door lock in Nigeria?

For a lock quoted at $3.00 FOB Yiwu, the landed cost in Lagos is roughly $4.20-4.40 per piece: about $0.20 ocean freight, $0.50 import duty, $0.28 VAT, plus clearing, terminal and documentation charges. Exact figures depend on the HS code, current tariff and the clearing agent's fees.

Why is the Lagos wholesale price so much higher than the FOB price?

Because the importer carries freight, duty, clearing, demurrage risk and working capital, and still needs a margin. Wholesale at $5.50-6.50 against a $4.20-4.40 landed cost is roughly 30-45% gross margin, which is normal for hardware distribution in Nigeria.

Is it cheaper to buy a container directly from China?

Yes, but only above a certain volume. A 20ft container of locks is roughly 15,000-20,000 pieces. Below that, sharing a mixed container through a consolidator is cheaper than paying for a full container yourself.
